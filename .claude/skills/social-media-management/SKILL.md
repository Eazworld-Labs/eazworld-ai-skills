# Social Media Management

## 1. Overview & Purpose

Runs the organic social presence end to end: channel selection, content calendars,
platform-native creation, scheduling, community management, and reporting. Target users
are brand and growth teams who need a consistent presence without daily improvisation.
Success is a repeatable system — a calendar that ships, replies that build trust, and
numbers that connect to business outcomes rather than vanity metrics.

## 2. Activation Conditions & Triggers

- **Trigger 1:** When a user asks to plan a content calendar, create posts, or schedule
  publishing across social platforms.
- **Trigger 2:** When growing a channel — followers, engagement, or profile-to-site
  traffic.
- **Trigger 3:** When managing comments, DMs, mentions, or a reputation-sensitive
  response situation.
- **Anti-Trigger:** Do not activate for paid campaigns (route to meta-ads or
  google-ads), email programs (route to email-marketing), or brand system definition
  (route to brand-identity).

## 3. Required Inputs & Project Audit

- **Mandatory Inputs:**
  1. The audience: which platforms they actually use and what they follow accounts for.
  2. Brand assets and approved claims — what may be posted without review.
  3. Capacity: how many posts per week can genuinely be produced and replied to.
- **Research & Audience Audit Checklist:**
  1. Read channel analytics for the last 90 days: what formats and topics performed.
  2. Audit competitor and adjacent accounts for format gaps, not for copying.
  3. List recurring content pillars (product, education, social proof, behind the
     scenes) and map them to the calendar.
  4. Check trending audio and formats per platform before committing the week's plan.

## 4. Technical Standards & Rules

- Follow each platform's community standards, advertising policies, and disclosure
  rules — sponsored content carries a clear disclosure tag.
- One account, one owner with two-factor authentication; access is granted through
  manager roles, never shared passwords.
- Every link carries a consistent UTM tag so social traffic is measurable in analytics.
- Content is platform-native: vertical video for Reels and TikTok, carousel for
  education, text-with-media for X and LinkedIn — cross-posting raw is discouraged.
- The calendar states pillar, format, hook, call to action, and owner per slot; a post
  without a call to action is decoration.
- Reply standards define tone, response time, and escalation — sensitive complaints move
  to private channels quickly.

Example of a calendar week:

```yaml
week: 2026-W45
posts:
  - day: Mon
    platform: instagram
    format: reel
    hook: "3 ways to style Ankara sneakers"
    cta: shop_link_in_bio
  - day: Wed
    platform: tiktok
    format: video
    hook: "How the team packs 100 orders a day"
    cta: comment_for_link
```

## 5. Execution Workflow (Step-by-Step)

1. **Step 1: Parse Task** — Restate the channels, the posting cadence, and the business
   goal the presence should serve.
2. **Step 2: Inspect Context** — Execute the audit checklist; report what the analytics
   already prove before planning new formats.
3. **Step 3: Formulate Plan** — Build the calendar: pillars across the week, formats per
   platform, and the hooks to lead with.
4. **Step 4: Verify Security** — Confirm account security (2FA, roles), that no
   credentials appear in any file, and that claims are approved.
5. **Step 5: Apply Edits** — Write the posts with captions, hashtags, and tagged links;
   schedule them through the chosen tool.
6. **Step 6: Execute Tests** — Preview every scheduled post on the actual device format,
   verify link tags resolve, and confirm disclosure tags on sponsored content.
7. **Step 7: Report Status** — Report what shipped, engagement signals, and the next
   calendar iteration based on data.

## 6. Security & Edge Case Guidelines

- **Account Security:** Two-factor authentication everywhere, manager-role access for
  collaborators, and access revoked when a collaborator leaves.
- **No Fake Growth:** Bought followers, engagement pods, and giveaway-follow schemes
  damage reach and violate platform rules — growth is earned through content.
- **Crisis Edge Cases:** A negative thread is acknowledged quickly, moved to a private
  channel, and answered once — coordinated replies from multiple accounts make it worse.
- **Privacy:** Never post customer messages, order details, or personal data as social
  proof without written consent.
- **Disclose:** Paid partnerships and gifted products are disclosed every time —
  undeclared ads are a legal risk in most markets.

## 7. Definition of Done & Quality Gates

- [ ] Calendar covers the planned weeks with pillar, format, hook, CTA, and owner.
- [ ] Every scheduled link carries consistent UTM tags and resolves correctly.
- [ ] Account security verified: 2FA on, roles instead of shared passwords.
- [ ] Reply standards and escalation path documented.
- [ ] KPIs defined with baseline and target — engagement rate, reach, follower growth,
      saves and shares, and profile-link clicks.
- [ ] Disclosure tags present on all sponsored content.
